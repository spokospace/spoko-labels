labels-render demo

Pick a made-up template, edit its data or its JSON, check the preview and print it on a NIIMBOT B1 (50x30 mm labels). Use Chrome or Edge, and close the NIIMBOT phone app first.

This browser has no Web Bluetooth: the preview works, printing does not. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android (Brave needs brave://flags/#brave-web-bluetooth-api).
